Cookie Policy

Information about how cookies and local storage are used on PianoSnap

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies and local storage items are small pieces of data stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit websites. They help the site recognize your device and remember your preferences or session state.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical Cookies

These cookies and storage items are essential for the site to function properly, remember your consent preferences, and authenticate administration sessions:

  • pianosnap_cookie_consent: remembers your cookie choice so you don't see the banner repeatedly.
  • ps_admin_token: temporary session token for administrative management.
  • pianosnap_songs: local browser cache used to provide fast, instantaneous song catalog loading.

3. Third-Party Content (YouTube)

PianoSnap embeds official YouTube video players to allow visitors to preview piano tutorials and listen to arrangements. When viewing embedded videos, YouTube / Google may set cookies or collect anonymous telemetry according to their privacy policy. You can block third-party cookies at any time via your browser settings.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can change or reset your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the button below or using the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.

Privacy Policy

How PianoSnap collects, uses, and protects your information

1. Contact & Data Controller

PianoSnap is committed to respecting user privacy. For any inquiries, feedback, or data requests, please contact us at: pianosnap.yt@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

PianoSnap is a free-access platform offering piano arrangements, MIDI files, and sheet music. We do not require regular users to create an account or provide personal information to browse or download. We only collect:

  • Basic technical network logs (standard server logs including anonymous IP address, browser type, timestamp) to prevent abuse and ensure security.
  • Local client-side configuration (such as your cookie preferences and song cache) stored solely on your own browser.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide you with fast catalog access, audio/video previews, and download links.
  • Ensure technical security, network stability, and protection against automated attacks.

4. Storage & Security

Catalog metadata is hosted on Google Cloud Firestore with enterprise-grade encryption in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest. No personal profiles, passwords, or payment cards are stored on our servers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request clarification regarding any data processed, to request deletion of cached records, and to manage your browser storage and cookies at any time. For questions, contact pianosnap.yt@gmail.com.
